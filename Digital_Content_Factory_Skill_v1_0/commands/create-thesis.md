# /create-thesis

Create a full thesis or dissertation from supplied research material, undergraduate through PhD. Portable variant — for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude without platform-specific output handling.

Use the Thesis/Dissertation Structure Engine (Nigerian university format by default, or the user's own supplied institutional template — never blended) and the Academic Citation & Verification Engine (APA 7 default, other styles selectable; compulsory source verification on every citation, with a verification summary at final QA). Confirm degree level before drafting and scale rigour accordingly. Build front matter, chapter-by-chapter body, and end matter per the confirmed skeleton. Obtain structural approval (chapter outline) before full drafting, per the skill's universal workflow. See `config/platform_notes.md` for output handling.
