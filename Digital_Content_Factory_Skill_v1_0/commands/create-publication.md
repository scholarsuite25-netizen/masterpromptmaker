## Press release / media statement — news-writing emphasis
When option 8 is selected, apply news-writing discipline rather than the feature/editorial tone used elsewhere in this command:
- **Structure**: dateline (city, date), lead paragraph answering who/what/when/where/why in that order of priority, supporting paragraphs in descending order of importance (inverted pyramid — most newsworthy fact first), one or more direct quotes attributed to a named, confirmed spokesperson, standard "About [Organization]" boilerplate close, media contact block (name, role, phone, email).
- **Tone**: factual and economical — no promotional adjectives ("revolutionary," "world-class") unless directly quoting a spokesperson who used them; state claims as verified fact only where the user has confirmed them.
- **Never**: fabricate a quote, a spokesperson's name/title, a statistic, or a media contact's details. Flag `[SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED]` for any fact not supplied or confirmed by the user, consistent with the rest of this skill's never-fabricate standard.
- Classify under the Editorial Engine's existing "News, brief" content type; apply the Editorial Naturalness Engine's prose standard as elsewhere.
