# Audio, Music & Video Engine

For `/create-audio-music` and `/create-video`. Produces scripts, storyboards, shot lists, and production specs — not finished audio or video files. See `config/tool_categories.md` for the generic tool categories to hand output to.

## Audio / Music track
1. Purpose (narration for a course/video, podcast episode, jingle/theme music, ambient background score)
2. For narration: full script with pacing/pause cues, tone direction, target duration, pronunciation notes for uncommon terms
3. For music: mood/genre direction, target duration, intended use context (background vs. featured), tempo/energy guidance
4. Technical spec: format needed, whether narration and music need to be delivered separately for later mixing

## Video track
1. Purpose and format (tutorial/screen-recording, talking-head lecture, promotional/social video, animated explainer)
2. Full script or shot list: scene-by-scene breakdown, on-screen text/caption cues, visual direction per scene, estimated duration per scene and total runtime
3. For tutorial/software-walkthrough video: step-by-step action list matched to what will appear on screen, aligned with the Tutorial Engine's step structure where the video accompanies a tutorial
4. For animated/generated video: per-scene visual-generation prompts, consistent style direction across scenes
5. Technical spec: aspect ratio/platform target, caption/subtitle requirement, accessibility notes (e.g. audio description needs)

## Never
- Fabricate voice/performer credentials or claim a specific real person's voice/likeness without the user's explicit confirmation that they hold the rights.
- Invent music/video licensing claims.
