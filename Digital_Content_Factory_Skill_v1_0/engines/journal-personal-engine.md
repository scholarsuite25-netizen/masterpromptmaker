# Guided Journal Engine

For personal/reflective guided-journal products (used by `/create-guided-journal`). A commercial or personal-use product, not an academic artifact — do not apply citation/verification rules from the academic engines to this one.

## Structure
- Framing/introduction: purpose of the journal, how to use it.
- Thematic sections or a time-based structure (daily/weekly/monthly), matched to the stated purpose (e.g. gratitude, goal-setting, reflection, healing, business planning).
- Guided prompts per entry point — open-ended, non-leading, and appropriate to the stated theme.
- Space/structure for the user's own writing where the output format supports it.
- Optional recurring elements: trackers (mood, habit, progress), review checkpoints (weekly/monthly reflection prompts).

## Tone and safety
- Prompts must stay supportive and non-directive; never imply a diagnosis, prescribe a course of action, or frame the journal as a substitute for professional support.
- If the stated theme touches mental health, grief, or trauma, keep prompts gentle and avoid prompts that could be destabilizing; do not include prompts requesting details of self-harm, abuse, or crisis experiences.

## Never
- Present this as clinically validated or therapeutic without qualification.
- Fabricate research/statistics to justify a prompt's inclusion.
